# claimsforce

> claimsforce GmbH is a Hamburg-based insurtech building AI-powered software for property and casualty claims management and underwriting. Its platform helps insurers, loss-adjusting and expert organizations, and independent adjusters digitize the end-to-end claims process. claimsforce is a private, login-only SaaS product with no public API or developer portal at this time; this profile is a company record in the API Evangelist network.

## Company
- Website: https://www.claimsforce.com
- About: https://www.claimsforce.com (Insurers, Adjuster/Expert organizations, Independent adjusters, Underwriting/Risk Management sections)
- Login: https://web.claimsforce.com/login

## Products
- Claims management for insurers (automated report and calculation generation, real-time claims analytics)
- Software for claims-adjuster and expert organizations (disposition planning, route/workload optimization)
- Tools for independent adjusters and experts (video-based remote case assessment)
- Underwriting and risk management
